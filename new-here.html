Welcome to the Family

New Here? We're Glad You're Here

Whether you're exploring faith, just resumed school, or looking for a place to belong — you are welcome at NIFES FUTMinna.

What to Expect

Visiting a new fellowship can feel intimidating. Here's a quick rundown so you know exactly what happens.

A Warm Welcome

From the moment you walk in, our members will greet you, help you find a seat, and make sure you feel at home. No one sits alone here.

Lively Worship

Our services begin with worship led by the Lord's Habitation Choir — a vibrant time of songs, praise, and thanksgiving to God.

Teaching from the Word

You'll hear a practical, scripture-based message that helps you apply God's Word to your life as a student.

No Pressure, No Strings

There's no embarrassing welcome moment or donation push. You're free to simply observe, participate as you feel led, and take your time.

Plan Your First Visit in 4 Easy Steps

  1. Pick a Service

    Choose any of our weekly gatherings above — Sunday Service is the best starting point for most first-time guests.

  2. Tell Us You're Coming

    Not required, but nice! Chat with us on WhatsApp and mention you're a first-timer — someone will look out for you.

  3. Arrive & Enjoy

    Come as you are — no dress code. Our welcome team will greet you, and you'll be seated right in time for worship.

  4. Connect After the Service

    Stick around! We'd love to meet you properly, share a bit about the fellowship, and help you find your place.
