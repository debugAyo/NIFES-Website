Trust & Transparency

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you use this website.

Our Commitment

NIFES FUTMinna respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we may collect through this website and how we use it. We keep things simple and only collect what we genuinely need to serve you.

Information We Collect

When you use our forms (e.g., Connect Form, Prayer Request), we collect the details you choose to provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, campus, department, and any message you share. When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.

We do not collect any information about you automatically beyond standard, anonymous website analytics (such as which pages are visited) that help us improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

The information you share is used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and connect you with the right fellowship leader.
  • Pray for you, in the case of prayer requests, and (if you consent) follow up with you.
  • Send you devotionals or updates you have subscribed to.
  • Improve our website and fellowship communication.

Prayer Requests & Anonymity

Prayer requests are handled confidentially by our prayer team. If you select the "anonymous" option, your request is shared without your name or contact details. We never publish your prayer requests publicly.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted third-party service providers (such as our form and email providers) strictly for the purpose of operating this website and communicating with you.

External Links

Our site links to external services such as WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Maps. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage only to remember your theme preference (e.g., dark or light mode) on this site. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for students and general audiences in tertiary institutions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in the emails we send. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us, please contact us via the details on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Effective date: August 8, 2026
